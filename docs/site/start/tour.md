Applies to nori 0.6 on Android. The other clients have the same pages in their own layout.

1. **Home** opens with a Continue card, then your shelves: mixes, recently played, newest and most
   played albums, top songs, random albums, favorites and the playlists you pinned. A ">" on a shelf
   opens its full list. Which rows show, and in what order, is yours to choose in Settings.
2. **Library** has a chip for each section: albums, artists, songs, playlists, genres, years, folders
   and radio. Favorites, downloads and history sit together in one card.
3. **Search** answers as you type, from an index kept on the phone, and adds the server's results when
   you pause. See [Library and search](../using/library-and-search.md).
4. **An album page** opens with its cover across the top, melting into a color taken from it. Play and
   Shuffle sit under the title; Download and Add to queue head the song list (a playlist also
   exports), each song has a menu, and a swipe on a row queues it or makes it a
   favorite.
5. **The player** opens from the bar at the bottom. Swipe the cover to skip, down to close. From there,
   Lyrics, rewind, play, fast-forward and Up next share one row; the output button, by the
   volume bar, opens the "Playing on" sheet to choose where the music plays.
6. **Settings** has a search field: type what you are after, such as "gapless" or "lyrics".
