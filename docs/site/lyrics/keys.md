---
title: Keys for lyrics services
description: The two lyrics services that work better, or at all, with your own key.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

Applies to nori 0.6.3 on Android, the desktop and the terminal.

Two services take a key of your own, in Settings, Lyrics, Lyrics sources:

| Key | What it adds |
|---|---|
| PaxSenix key | PaxSenix's Musixmatch and Spotify lyrics. These two are never on without it: switching either on asks for the key, and saving it turns that service on. Cancel and it stays off. |
| BetterLyrics key | lyrics for songs BetterLyrics has not stored yet. Without one it answers only for songs it already has. Entered under Keys on the same page. |

Once the PaxSenix key is given, its services' rows say so and offer Change key. Saving it empty
removes the key and turns both services off.

Keys are kept with your settings on the device and sent only to their own service.
